Privacy Policy for ZenShield

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

ZenShield ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ZenShield mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

ZenShield is designed with a privacy-first approach.

  • Notification Data: We monitor incoming notifications to filter distractions based on your settings. This processing happens entirely on your device. We do not transmit your notification content to any external servers.
  • Local Storage: Blocked notification logs (app name, title, and message) are stored locally in a secure Room database on your device. You can clear this history or export it at any time.
  • Permissions: We require the "Notification Listener" permission to function and "Ignore Battery Optimizations" for service reliability. These permissions are used solely for the core functionality of the app.

2. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal data with third parties. All distraction-filtering logic and data storage remain local to your hardware.

3. Security

We prioritize the security of your local data by leveraging Android's native sandboxing and encrypted storage primitives.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@zenshield.app
